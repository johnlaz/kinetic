What it does

Real covariance math, not a spreadsheet guess.

Efficient frontier

2,000 simulated portfolios with optimal candidates picked out for you.

Monte Carlo

Ten-year paths with median and 90% confidence band per strategy.

Stress tests

Oil shock, equity crash, Fed hike, tech crash and eight more scenarios.

Income floor

Monthly and annual cash flow, tax drag and a 20-year projection.

Rebalance

Drift monitoring and suggested trades against locked targets.

AI scan

Optional second opinion from Groq (free) or xAI using your own key.

How it works

Three steps, no account.

Set weights

Build a strategy with sliders, presets or a CSV import.

Read the model

Return, volatility, Sharpe and income update as you move.

Stress it

Run scenarios and the frontier, then compare strategies side by side.

Private by design. Portfolios and keys stay in your browser. There is no server, account or tracking. Optional AI and market-data calls go straight from your browser to the provider you choose.

Install it

Works offline after the first load.

  1. Desktop (Chrome/Edge): open the app and click the install icon in the address bar.
  2. Android: menu → Add to Home screen.
  3. iPhone/iPad: Share → Add to Home Screen.
